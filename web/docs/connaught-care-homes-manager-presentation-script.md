# Connaught Care Homes Manager Presentation Script

## Short Opening

I wanted to show you a recipe app I have built to improve how our chefs access and use recipes day to day.

This is not intended to replace Saffron.
Saffron would still remain the company system for procurement and ordering.

What this app does is provide a simpler, faster, more chef-friendly way to browse and use recipes in practice.

## The Problem

At the moment, we already have the information in Saffron, but from a chef point of view it can be quite tedious to use.

That matters because chefs need something practical during prep and service, not just a system that stores the information.

So the gap is not that the company has no system.
The gap is that the current system is not ideal as a working kitchen recipe tool.

## The Solution

This app gives chefs:

- easier recipe browsing
- quicker search and filtering
- a clearer way to see ingredients and method
- access to allergens, nutrition, and costing in a much more usable layout
- a mobile-friendly experience that is easier to use in real kitchen conditions

It also gives managers and owners:

- better control over who can access recipes
- visibility management for recipe collections
- a cleaner subscriber and access workflow
- a stronger platform for standardising recipes across homes

## Why It Matters To Connaught Care Homes

The value is practical.

If chefs can find the right recipe faster, understand it more quickly, and work from a consistent format, that improves day-to-day kitchen efficiency.

Across 6 homes, even small time savings and small reductions in friction become meaningful.

It also helps with:

- consistency between homes
- onboarding new chefs or cover staff
- reducing reliance on memory or informal workarounds
- making recipe information easier to trust and use

## Why This Does Not Conflict With Saffron

I would position this as a working layer for chefs, not as a replacement system.

So the message is:

Saffron remains the required procurement and ordering platform.
This app becomes the chef-facing recipe platform that is easier to use operationally.

That means the company can gain value without needing to replace its existing contract.

## Why A Monthly Fee Makes Sense

This would not just be a one-off build.

The monthly fee would cover:

- maintenance
- support
- bug fixes
- hosting
- ongoing improvements
- recipe data updates
- future features such as stronger reporting, nutrition, and costing workflows

So the fee would reflect ongoing operational value, not just the initial build.

## Simple Closing

My proposal is not to replace what the company already uses.
It is to give Connaught Care Homes a more practical recipe tool for chefs that improves usability, consistency, and day-to-day efficiency.

If you think it is worth exploring, I would be happy to show how it works in practice and discuss whether it could be useful across the homes.
