# Connaught Care Homes Chef App Value Summary

## Purpose

This app is not intended to replace Saffron.  
Saffron remains the company's formal procurement and ordering system.

The purpose of this app is to give chefs a faster, clearer, more practical way to access and use recipes day to day.

## The Problem Today

Connaught Care Homes already has a working procurement process through Saffron, and the company is committed to using it.
However, from a chef's point of view, Saffron is not an efficient working recipe tool.

Common day-to-day issues are:

- Finding recipes quickly can feel slow and tedious.
- Recipe browsing is not designed around kitchen flow and service pressure.
- Costing may exist in the system, but it is not always easy to access in a practical chef-friendly way.
- New or covering chefs can take longer to find the right recipe information.
- Standardisation across homes is harder when the recipe experience is clunky to use.

## What This App Adds

This app adds a chef-friendly operational layer on top of the existing company setup.

It gives chefs:

- A much simpler recipe browsing experience
- Clear ingredient and method presentation
- Faster search and filtering
- Saved favourites for commonly used recipes
- Better visibility of portions, allergens, nutrition, and costing
- Consistent recipe access on web and mobile-friendly layouts

For owners and managers, it gives:

- Visibility control over which recipes are available
- A cleaner owner dashboard for recipe management
- Subscriber access management
- Enterprise/public access separation
- A foundation for standardised costing and nutrition workflows

## True Business Value

### 1. Saves Chef Time

The biggest practical value is time.
If chefs can reach the right recipe faster and understand it more easily, that reduces friction every day.

Even small time savings matter when repeated across:

- 6 working care homes
- multiple chefs
- daily breakfast, lunch, supper, snacks, and special diet preparation

### 2. Improves Consistency Across Homes

When everyone uses the same recipe presentation and the same approved records, there is less variation between homes.
That supports:

- more consistent meal output
- easier staff handover
- easier cover between homes
- simpler onboarding of new chefs

### 3. Reduces Training and Onboarding Friction

New chefs, agency chefs, and support staff need a system that is quick to understand.
This app reduces the effort needed to learn where information lives.

That is especially valuable in care settings where continuity and clarity matter.

### 4. Gives Chefs a Tool They Will Actually Want to Use

This is important.
The company may be contracted into Saffron, but adoption and day-to-day usefulness are separate questions.

If chefs prefer using this app for practical recipe access, the business gains value even while Saffron remains the official back-office system.

### 5. Creates a Better Foundation for Operational Control

The app already supports or is moving toward:

- recipe visibility management
- costing visibility
- nutrition visibility
- owner control over recipe access
- subscriber and enterprise permission management

That creates a platform the company can continue to improve over time without changing procurement provider.

## Why This Is Not Competing With Saffron

This should be presented as a complementary tool.

Suggested positioning:

> Saffron remains the ordering and procurement system.  
> This app is the chef-use system for recipe access, clarity, consistency, and day-to-day kitchen efficiency.

That makes the proposal easier to accept internally because it is not asking the company to replace an existing contract.

## Why a Monthly Fee Is Reasonable

A monthly fee is justified because this is not just a one-off build.
It provides continuing operational value and requires ongoing support.

The monthly fee can be framed around:

- hosting and uptime
- bug fixes and maintenance
- recipe data improvements
- ongoing feature refinement
- access management and support
- security updates
- future additions such as stronger reporting, costing, nutrition, and chef workflow improvements

## Suggested Manager Pitch

You could present it like this:

> We already have Saffron for procurement and ordering, and I am not proposing we replace it.  
> What I am proposing is a simpler chef-facing recipe platform that makes day-to-day kitchen work faster and easier.  
> It improves recipe access, consistency, onboarding, and visibility of important information like portions, allergens, costing, and nutrition.  
> Across our homes, that creates practical value for chefs and stronger consistency for the business.

## Recommended First Outcome

The first internal success measure does not need to be complicated.

It can simply be:

- chefs can find recipes faster
- chefs prefer using the app for recipe access
- recipe information is clearer during prep and service
- managers can see that the app supports consistency across homes

## Conclusion

The value of this app is not that it replaces procurement software.
The value is that it gives Connaught Care Homes a chef-friendly operational recipe platform that is easier to use, easier to adopt, and more practical in real kitchen conditions.

That is where the business case sits.
